An original card game designed and prototyped for the 2026 Qubit Spring Card Game Design Competition, winner of the Grand Prize among 33 professional and hobbyist entries. Biobot explores the symbiosis of organic tissue and robotics through a tableau-building system of transparent, stackable cards, praised for its innovative systemic mechanics and elegant, intuitive UX.
